### Research interests

- Aerodynamics of vertical-axis and floating offshore wind turbines
- Turbulence modelling and Lagrangian particle transport
- Machine-learning surrogate models for CFD

### M.Sc. thesis

*Numerical investigation of the local aerodynamic coefficients of a floating
offshore vertical-axis wind turbine and development of a machine-learning
surrogate model for combined platform and blade motions* (expected 2027).
Supervisor: Prof. Reza Kamali · Advisor: Dr. Ebrahim Ghahramani

### Research software

**[crw-dehbi-2008-fluent-udf](https://github.com/sina-karbakhsh/crw-dehbi-2008-fluent-udf)**<br>
Compiled ANSYS Fluent UDF (C) implementing the CRW–Langevin particle dispersion
model of Dehbi (2008), applied to aerosol deposition in a 90° pipe bend, with a
technical report and a statistical verification of the random forcing.<br>
*Reynolds stress model · Lagrangian particle tracking · C · Python*
